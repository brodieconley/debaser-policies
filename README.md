# Debaser — Open Policy Documents

[![License: CC BY-SA 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC%20BY--SA%204.0-lightgrey.svg)](LICENSE)

We are [Debaser](https://debaser.ca) — an Ottawa-based music presenter and arts not-for-profit rooted in Anishinaabe-Algonquin territory. We present experimental, community-driven music and art, and we care a lot about how organizations like ours are run.

These are our organizational policy documents, published openly. We're sharing them because we know how hard it can be to write these things from scratch — especially for small, under-resourced arts organizations. Take them, adapt them, make them yours.

## Why We're Sharing This

Policy documents are often treated as proprietary — but for small nonprofits, the hardest part of governance isn't the ideas, it's having a starting point. We've spent real time developing these policies to reflect our values, and we think they could be a useful foundation for other organizations, especially in arts and music.

We're sharing them under [CC BY-SA 4.0](LICENSE), which means you can freely adapt and redistribute them — as long as you credit us and share your version under the same terms.

## How to Use These Policies

1. **Fork this repository** on GitHub (or just download the files)
2. **Replace "Debaser" with your organization's name** throughout all documents
3. **Update the dates, governance titles, and contact information** to match your org
4. **Review each policy** for content that may not fit your context — some clauses reflect our specific values and jurisdiction
5. **Adopt with your board** and update the `Effective Date` fields accordingly

For a full step-by-step guide — including instructions for people who've never used GitHub — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

### Quick Adapter's Checklist

- [ ] Replace "Debaser" with your organization's name throughout
- [ ] Update all `Effective Date:` fields to your board adoption date
- [ ] Adjust governance titles (Board Chair, Vice-Chair) to match your structure
- [ ] Review Conflict of Interest definitions for "Members" and "participants"
- [ ] Partnership Policy — PACBI clause: keep, remove, or replace with your own criteria
- [ ] Check for Ontario/Canada-specific language and adapt for your jurisdiction
- [ ] Replace Debaser contact info with your own
- [ ] Rewrite the Mandate and Vision to reflect your organization's goals

Full checklist with explanations: [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)
