##### **GENERAL TERMS OF POLICY** *(scope/ability) (MVV reinforcement)*

1.  *Definition of a partner*

> Debaser is committed to maintaining partnerships with organizations
> that support and/or reflect Debaser's mandate, vision, and core
> values, as elaborated above, either explicitly or implicitly.
> Additionally, a potential partner supports and aligns with Debaser's
> other policies, including its Safer Spaces, Accessibility, and
> Diversity, Equity and Inclusion policies.

2.  *Values informing partnerships*

> As an arts presenter and community partner, Debaser's work is informed
> by the organization's mandate and vision statement.
>
> Debaser is committed to creating inclusive and responsive community
> spaces that cultivate shared understanding, joy, and social impact. We
> follow a care-centered approach that promotes safety, accessibility,
> and anti-oppression, and supports self-directed forms of
> participation. We work to make sure that all aspects of Debaser's work
> align with our core values: respect, equity, inclusivity, consent,
> safety, well-being, and self-determination.
>
> There are two types of entities that Debaser partners with:
>
> *2.2.a Community partner*
>
> A Community partner is a non-profit organization, unincorporated
> ad-hoc collective, or individual whose mandate and vision is aligned
> with those of Debaser. Partnership with a Community partner may
> benefit either or both organizations by sharing resources, supporting
> outreach, or otherwise broadening impact.
>
> *2.2.b For-profit partner*
>
> A for-profit partner is a for-profit business or corporation. A
> for-profit partner may benefit from partnership with Debaser for
> marketing or outreach purposes, while Debaser may benefit from
> partnership with a for-profit partner through cash, in-kind
> contributions, support for outreach, and broadened impact.

3.  *Partnership Criteria*

> Debaser is interested in partnerships with organizations,
> institutions, collectives and individuals that:

1.  Offer resources that support the fulfillment of Debaser's mandate
    and vision; and/or

2.  Undertake activities that support the fulfillment of Debaser's
    mandate and vision; and/or

3.  Stand to benefit from resources that Debaser can offer to it.

##### **PARTNERSHIP CONDITIONS**

1.  General conditions of Debaser partnerships are determined through
    the negotiation of a partnership agreement, created by both parties.

2.  Debaser requires its partners to provide transparency on their
    operations, governance, and finances (i.e. investors or investments)
    upon request to ensure that they support and align with Debaser's
    mandate and vision, core values, and policies.

3.  Debaser is committed to upholding the principles of PACBI as defined
    on the PACBI website, outlined
    [here](https://bdsmovement.net/pacbi). Please refer to
    our statement
    [here](https://www.debaser.ca/news/statement-pacbi-arms-embargo-now).

4.  A non-disclosure clause is included in and applicable to all Debaser
    partnership agreements, and can vary in severity and confidentiality
    by and can vary in conditions based on the context of the
    partnership. Financial and accounting elements of the agreement are
    subject to non-disclosure, as well as but not limited to
    intellectual property, business secrets, and internal processes of
    operations.

5.  Debaser and its partners are to respect each other's right to
    determine what is best for their own organization or group, as long
    as their statements and actions do not perpetuate harm or constitute
    racism, misogyny, homophobia, transphobia, ableism, or other forms
    of oppression.

6.  Board Committees such as Policy, Treasury and Human Resources are
    capable of interceding within the scope of their purview, and are
    entitled to overseeing the development of partnerships at any given
    time within the process. Provisions and recommendations from
    aforementioned committees will be held in account at the discretion
    of Debaser staff throughout this process.

##### **DEBASER PARTNERSHIP RESPONSIBILITIES**

1.  Debaser's role and responsibilities for partnerships are outlined
    within the partnership agreement, which is signed by both parties.
    Debaser will hold itself accountable to the terms of individual
    partnerships via the parameters outlined within the partnership
    agreement, and more foundationally through its mandate and vision
    (See section 2).

##### **PARTNER RESPONSIBILITIES**

1.  The Partner is to follow and adhere to the outlined roles and
    responsibilities within the signed partnership agreement. The
    Partner will deliver the expected goods and services that were
    negotiated and agreed upon in the agreement to the best of their
    ability, and ought to be delivered and completed as outlined within
    the agreement.

2.  The Partner is responsible for ensuring that the quality of the
    goods and services offered in the partnership agreement meet the
    same quality and standard of their non-partnered services. This
    includes but is not limited to rentals, usage of facilities,
    skillsharing, labour, and human resources.

##### **PROCEDURES**

1.  Amendments to partnership agreements may be made via mutual
    agreement of both parties.

2.  Process for Renewal of Partnerships

    1.  A review of the previous agreement and assessment of partnership
        health and suitability shall be conducted prior to the renewal
        of partnership agreements between Debaser and a current Partner.
        The Debaser Board of Directors are capable of interceding within
        the scope of their purview, and are entitled to overseeing the
        development of partnerships at any given time within the process
        at their discretion.

    2.  Completion of the roles and responsibilities outlined in the
        partnership agreement by both parties is grounds for renewal.

    3.  Failure to uphold the roles and responsibilities outlined in the
        partnership agreement, from either or both parties, is grounds
        for suspension or termination of the partnership.

3.  Termination of Partnership

    1.  Termination of an agreement is contingent on adherence to the
        obligations outlined in the partnership agreement.

    2.  Immediate termination can be invoked at the discretion of
        Debaser staff or Board of Directors if the Partner violates
        Debaser's Code of Conduct as outlined in the Partnership
        Agreement, or evidence of the Partner participating in
        malpractice, harmful behaviour, or illicit activity.
