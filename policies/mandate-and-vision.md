**Debaser Mandate and Vision**

Effective Date: 2026-01-20

Date Last Reviewed: 2026-02-20

Scheduled Review Date: 2028-02-20

Approved by: Debaser Board of Directors

**MANDATE:**

Debaser exists to...

1)  **Catalyze innovative and collaborative music and artistic projects
    via:**

    a)  Supporting artists at creation, production and presentation
        stages through commissions, residencies, exhibitions, and
        curated programming;

    b)  Collaborating with local, national, and international partners
        to support the development of projects that serve common goals
        while multiplying community and organizational impacts;

    c)  Music and artistic programming that is motivated by curation and
        community responsiveness.

2)  **Support underrepresented, equity-deserving, and outsider art and
    artists through:**

    a)  Supporting artists from or based on Anishinabe-Algonquin
        territory in the land colonially known as canada;

    b)  Prioritizing underrepresented and equity-deserving groups
        including but not limited to Indigenous, Black, people of
        colour, disabled, neurodivergent, displaced, 2SLGBTQIA+, women,
        and gender nonconforming artists;

    c)  Prioritizing artists that work within alternative or
        "do-it-yourself" models of artistic and/or economic development,
        production, presentation, and dissemination;

    d)  Presenting musical performances and artworks that are
        artist-driven, visionary, experimental, community-engaged and/or
        created to incite social change.

3)  **Create inclusive and responsive community spaces that cultivate
    shared understanding, joy, and social impact by:**

    a)  Following a care-centered approach for our community that
        promotes safety, accessibility, and anti-oppression, and
        supports self-directed forms of participation;

    b)  Facilitating curation and discovery with an anti-colonial
        approach by prioritizing artistic self-determination and
        community responsiveness;

    c)  Creating networks between local, national and international
        organizations for the benefit of artists, audiences and
        organizations.

**OUR VISION FOR DEBASER IN 8 YEARS:**

**...as a music and arts presenter**

- An internationally recognized music and arts presenter based on
  Anishinaabe-Algonquin territory, and trusted for its curation of
  diverse, experimental art and artists.

- An organization committed to curatorial, artistic and structural
  experimentation and innovation that is nourishing and responsive to
  the needs of the community.

- A builder of an arts scene where members have a deeper, richer, and
  more adventurous understanding and appreciation of music and art.

- A magnet for adventurous audiences from around the world.

**...as a community builder**

- A catalyst for the continued growth of a flourishing local underground
  music and arts community that encompasses a variety of mediums and
  genres.

- A supporter of a thriving, more equitable artistic ecosystem where
  artists feel valued and that their career in the arts is sustainable.

- A trusted partner of organizations at multiple levels, from grassroots
  collectives dedicated to social change to national art institutions
  and international presenters.

- An organizer that accommodates the diverse access needs and
  prioritizes the individual agency of community members through
  thoughtful programming, responsive planning, and continuous learning
  and unlearning.

- A key node in a network of avant-garde music and arts presenters
  across Turtle Island that strengthens and supports artistic careers
  through active sharing of resources, collaborative initiatives, and
  structural innovation.

**...as a not-for-profit employer in the arts**

- A sustainable arts employer and modeler of progressive employment
  practices whose activities support broader local creative economies.

- A steward of emerging talent in the arts through hands-on mentoring,
  experiential learning, and knowledge exchange.

- A model of a respectful and equitable working environment in the arts,
  and proponent of artistic accommodations that appropriately recognize
  the value of artistic labour.

- A leader for "DIY"-driven, transparent, equitable, and sustainable
  non-profit governance models.
