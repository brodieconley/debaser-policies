## Preamble

This *Conflict of Interest Policy (the "Policy")* for Debaser is
designed to protect Debaser's interests, and reflect and extend its
mandate and vision, when it is contemplating entering into or continuing
a transaction or arrangement that might benefit the private interest of
a Debaser Board Director, Officer, or a Committee member (together,
referred to as as *"Members"* throughout this document.).

Members shall not engage in any transaction or arrangement with Debaser
that involves an actual or perceived conflict of interest, except in
compliance with the Policy.

The Policy is intended to supplement, but not replace, any applicable
provincial or federal laws governing conflicts of interests applicable
to Debaser.

The question of conflict of interest cannot be perfectly resolved by a
list of do\'s and don\'ts. Conflict of interest goes beyond meeting
minimum legal requirements and must include ethical considerations. The
identification of a situation where a conflict of interest may exist
will depend on how the person involved perceives the situation, and how
their actions appear to others. Simply stated it is a matter of
conscience, service to Debaser's community, including artists and the
public, and common sense.

When viewed in this context, Members must be seen to be making decisions
which are free from personal bias. At the same time, it is considered
desirable to have Members who are interested and involved in the
Ottawa-region and broad Canadian music ecosystems. It is important to
balance this appreciation and direct involvement in these music
ecosystems with the need of Members to be conscious of their visible
roles with the general public, and the music and arts communities.

To assist the Members in performing the duties required of them by
Debaser or provincial and federal law with respect to their oversight
responsibilities and fiduciary obligations to Debaser and to the public,
the Debaser Board of Directors adopts the following Policy by
resolution:

## Purpose

All Members have a duty to ensure that the integrity of the
decision-making processes of the

Debaser Board and Committees are maintained by ensuring that they and
other Members are free from conflict or potential conflict in their
decision-making. It is important that all Members ---including
Directors, Officers and Committee members---understand their obligations
when a conflict of interest or potential conflict of interest arises,
and act with integrity to navigate any conflicts. It is, for example,
inherent in a Member's fiduciary duty that conflicts of interest be
avoided.

## Scope

This Policy applies to all Debaser Board Directors and Officers, and all
non-Board Committee Members (together, referred to as *"Members"*).

"Directors" means those individuals elected to the Debaser Board through
the official Director election process, as defined in the Debaser
By-Laws.

"Officers" means Officers appointed by the Board including the Chair, a
Vice-Chair, Secretary, Treasurer, and others who perform functions for
the organisation similar to those normally performed by such Officers.

"Committee Members" means those individuals who take part in formal and
ad-hoc committees convened by the Debaser Board of Directors or staff,
and who play a part in directly supporting Debaser operations.

## Conflict of Interest Policy

Members shall avoid situations in which they may be in a position of
conflict of interest or perceived conflict of interest. The process set
out in this policy shall be followed when a conflict or potential
conflict arises, alongside all responsibilities and fiduciary duties
related to conflict of interest required by provincial and federal law.

### Description of Conflict of Interest

A conflict of interest occurs when a Member's responsibility to act in
the best interest of Debaser and follow duties (for Board Directors and
Officers) is affected or hindered by another interest, relationship, or
obligation of the Member.

A conflict of interest also occurs when a Member\'s responsibilities to
Debaser are in conflict with other obligations the Member has,
preventing them from fully meeting their duties to Debaser.

The situations in which potential conflict of interest may arise cannot
be exhaustively set out.

Conflicts generally arise in the following situations:

#### A. Transacting with the Organization

When a Member transacts with Debaser directly or indirectly (a
transaction may include a financial transaction, trade, quid pro quo,
favour, or other form of exchange). When a Member has a material, direct
or indirect, interest in a transaction or contract with Debaser. For
example, when a member is under consideration to be booked as an artist
or hired to provide a service for Debaser.

#### B. Interest of a Relative

When Debaser conducts business or transacts with suppliers of goods or
services, or any other party of which a relative or individual in a
Member\'s household is a principal, officer, or representative. For
example, when a Member's spouse is under consideration to be booked as
an artist, or when a Member's child is applying for employment at
Debaser.

#### C. Gifts

When a Member, relative, or individual in their household receives
gifts, payments, services, or anything else of significant value from a
person or organisation that does business with Debaser. This applies if
the gift or benefit is intended to, or could be seen as trying to,
influence a decision or action of the Board. For example, if a local
artist presented a Member with a gift, while also indicating that they
have applied to perform at a Debaser event.

#### D. Appropriation of Organization Opportunity

When a Member diverts to the Member's own use, an opportunity or
advantage that

belongs to Debaser. For example, if a Member is in charge of booking an
artist for a Debaser event, there would be a conflict of interest if,
instead of booking the artist for Debaser, the Member books them for a
separate event they are organising for their own business.

#### E. Duty to Disclose Information of Value to the Organization

When a Member fails to disclose information that, if withheld, would
cause financial, legal or reputational harm, or otherwise fail to
promote the best interests of Debaser?

#### F. Serving on the Board of Other Organizations / Employment with Other Organisations

A Member may be in several situations of conflict of interest. This may
arise where the Member serves as a Member of two organisations that are
competing or transacting with one another (i.e. serving for both Debaser
and another competing organisation). It may also arise where a Member
has an association or relationship with another entity.

### Perceived Conflicts

There may be cases where the perception of a conflict of interest may be
harmful to Debaser, even where no formal conflict exists or has
occurred. While these situations are difficult to exhaustively
enumerate, this policy requires all Members to evaluate their actions
and activities relative to their potential for perceived harm to
Debaser. In such circumstances where they believe there to be a
potential for the perception that their actions create a conflict of
interest, the process set out in this policy for addressing conflicts
shall be followed.

### Disclosure of Conflicts

A Member who is in a position of conflict, potential conflict, or
perceived conflict shall immediately disclose such conflict to the Board
by notification to the Chair or Vice-Chair of the Board. Where the Chair
has a conflict, notice shall be given to the Vice-Chair. If the
Vice-Chair has a conflict, then notice shall be given to another member
of the Board who is without a conflict. The disclosure shall be
sufficient to disclose the nature and extent of the interest. Disclosure
shall be made at the earliest possible time and, where possible, prior
to any discussion and vote on the matter.

Where (i) a Member is not present at a meeting where a matter in which
the Member has a

conflict is first discussed and/or voted upon, or (ii) a conflict arises
for a Member after a matter has been discussed but not yet voted upon by
the Board, or (iii) a Member becomes conflicted after a matter has been
approved, the Member shall make the declaration of the conflict to the
Chair or Vice-chair as soon as possible, and at the next meeting of the
Board.

If a Member becomes interested in a contract or transaction after it is
made or entered into, the disclosure shall be made as soon as possible
after the Member becomes so interested.

A Member may also proactively make a general declaration of the Member's
relationships and interests in entities or persons that may give rise to
conflicts, allowing for the identification of potential Member conflicts
prior to Debaser initiating any relationships, activities or contracts
with entities or persons that may give rise to conflict.

### Abstain from Discussions

The Member who has declared a conflict shall not be present during the
discussion or vote in respect of the matter in which the Member has a
conflict and shall not attempt in any way to influence the voting.

### Process for Resolution of Conflicts of Interest

All Members shall comply with the requirements of the Debaser bylaws and
applicable provincial and federal law.

A Member may be referred to the process outlined below in any of the
following circumstances:

#### A. Circumstances for Referral

Where any Member believes that they or another Member:

I.  Is in a situation of actual, potential, or perceived conflict of
    interest; or

II. Has behaved or is likely to behave in a manner that is not
    consistent with the highest standards of trust and integrity and
    such behaviour may have an adverse impact on Debaser.

#### B. Process for Resolution

The matter shall be referred to the following process:

A.  COI is declared to the Board Chair through a simplified reporting
    template (*see APPENDIX B: Debaser Conflict of Interest Reporting
    Form*), or where the issue may involve the Chair, to the Vice-Chair.

B.  The Board Chair (or Vice-Chair, as the case may be) shares the
    report with the rest of the Board of Directors, not including the
    person declaring the potential conflict of interest, and provides
    one week (seven days) for the Board to review.

C.  As part of this review, any Member of the Board of Directors may
    request a formal meeting of the Board to consider the potential
    conflict of interest in question, or bring their concerns over the
    conflict in question to the Board via e-mail. Whether a potential
    conflict of interest warrants discussion at the Board level should
    be judged based on the complexity of the matter at hand with respect
    to balancing harms to Debaser against reasons and mitigating factors
    for the conflict, and whether there is past precedent for the Board
    making decisions on similar cases.

D.  If one or more Board Members request a formal meeting or initiate
    discussion over the conflict via e-mail, then a full Board
    discussion will take place - either via special meeting (scheduled
    as soon as possible) or through email communications - to consider
    the issue as soon as possible. It may also be discussed at the next
    monthly Board meeting. A decision on whether to take action with
    respect to the conflict of interest in question will be made at that
    meeting or via email, with a majority vote of the Board required for
    any action decided.

E.  If no Board member requests a formal meeting or raises concerns
    during the review period, the Chair (or Vice-Chair, as the case may
    be) will make a unilateral decision on any action to be taken in
    response to the potential conflict of interest in question.

F.  In cases where the Chair (or Vice-Chair, as the case may be) make a
    unilateral decision with respect to a conflict of interest (as per
    the process above), the matter(s) in question must be reported in
    full at the next Board meeting following the resolution of the
    matter. This provision enables the full Board to be aware of all
    Conflict of Interest related undertakings, and to properly exercise
    their fiduciary duties as Directors.

G.  A report on the Conflict of Interest in question, the process for
    handling it, and reasoning for any action taken in response is
    prepared by the Chair (with support from Debaser staff, if
    requested) and kept on file in Debaser's official records.

H.  It is recognized that if a conflict, or other matter referred cannot
    be resolved to the satisfaction of the Board (by simple majority
    resolution), a Member may be asked to resign or may be subject to
    removal pursuant to the [Debaser
    bylaws](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1df5IhrdAmUnubC4rgDCaVfheqVHTr3Jn/view?usp=sharing)
    and applicable legislation.

#### C. Considerations and Remedies

The purpose of this conflict of interest policy is to protect the
interests of Debaser while supporting its mandate and vision, rather
than to be punitive. When addressing and adjudicating a conflict of
interest, the Board will seek solutions that address the conflict while
minimizing negative impacts on the member involved. All remedies will be
designed to safeguard Debaser\'s integrity and operational
effectiveness, minimize any real or reputational harm, and support the
organisation's mandate and vision. Additionally, remedies must consider
fairness and proportionality in relation to the Member\'s role, as well
as the possibility that Debaser's broader organisational goals may
supersede the conflict in question.

As an example, a Member performing at a Debaser event represents a
potential conflict of interest. However, adjudicating this conflict
involves weighing this risk of harm to Debaser against the potential
benefits of allowing Members to perform at Debaser events (while
forfeiting any financial compensation for their performance). These
potential benefits reflect the values elaborated in the Preamble to this
policy, which highlights the desirability of having Members who are
interested and involved in the Ottawa-region and broad Canadian music
ecosystems. Fully disallowing any Members from performing at Debaser
events might disincentive strong candidates from applying to join or
sitting on the Debaser Board. Of course, this represents a non-specific
example to demonstrate a potential way to balance harms and benefits.
All conflicts should be judged individually based on their unique facts.

Possible outcomes of the conflict resolution process may include, but
are not limited to:

- Permitting the situation to proceed despite the potential conflict;

- Requiring the member to modify their actions to avoid the conflict;

- Requesting the Member's resignation from the Board of Directors or
  other positions as necessary to resolve the conflict.

#### C. Appeal

Within fifteen (15) days of receiving notice of the determination that
an interest poses an actual, potential, or perceived conflict, and a
ruling on how to address that conflict, a Member may appeal the
determination by submitting a written request for review to the Board.
The Board shall have sole discretion as to whether and how to review any
such determination, and all decisions of the Board shall be final and
not subject to further appeal.

### Records

The records of any Board or Committee meeting or other correspondence in
which a conflict of interest is discussed shall contain all of the
following:

- the name of the Member who disclosed a potential conflict of interest
  or was otherwise found to have a conflict of interest;

- the nature of the conflict of interest;

- if necessary, any action taken to determine whether a conflict of
  interest was present;

- the decision as to whether a conflict of interest existed; and a
  decision on whether the transaction or matter in question shall be
  approved or proceed notwithstanding the conflict;

- the names of the persons who were present for, or otherwise
  participated in, discussions and votes relating to the transaction or
  arrangement, and a record of any votes taken in connection with the
  proceedings.

All records related to conflict of interest shall be archived as part of
Debaser's official records.

### Member Assent

Each Member shall, upon joining the Debaser Board or a Debaser
Committee, sign a statement which affirms such person (a) has received a
copy of the Policy, (b) has read and understands the Policy, and (c)
agrees to comply with the Policy (see: APPENDIX B: Debaser Conflict of
Interest Acknowledgement Letter).
